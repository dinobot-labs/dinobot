# Reference research guide (groups A, B, C)

Each group delivers at least 9 accepted entries (target 10); together at least 20. Pools do not overlap. Swaps from godly.website, awwwards SOTD, land-book or lapa.ninja are allowed if you record the reason.

Tools: node scripts/shot.mjs <url> <prefix> --scrolls=2 [--mobile]; node scripts/fingerprint.mjs <url>. LOOK at every screenshot with Read before writing.

Output: docs/references/<group>.md and docs/references/shots/<group>-NN-slug-k.jpg (at most 250 KB each).

Entry template:
### A01. Name: https://example.com
- Category:
- Verified: <date>, HTTP 200, shots: ...
- Fingerprint: fonts; ink/bg; H1 px/tracking; motion tech; transfer KB; scroll min-window fps
- Why it's great (2-4 specific bullets)
- Color | Type | Layout | Motion | Mascot
- Borrow (2-4 concrete bullets)
- Avoid

End with "Group takeaways" (6-10 token-level rules) and "Top 3".
