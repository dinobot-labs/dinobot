Signal Paper

The system behind the dinobot landing page. Paper and ink, five flat signals used one at a time, and a single dot that builds everything from the mascot to the loaders.

01 Principles

Fifteen rules. Each one earned from a reference.

Derived from 35 documented sites (R01-R35 in docs/references.md). When a decision is unclear, these settle it.

  1. 01

    Paper first

    About 90% of every screen is neutral: paper, white surfaces, ink, and grays from one cool hue.

    R01 R02 R05 R10 R12

  2. 02

    One signal per screen

    The five brand stops are flat fills. A section opts into one; text in that color uses its deep variant.

    R13 R14 R15 R29 R35

  3. 03

    The primary action is ink

    One filled CTA style, an ink pill. Secondary is paper with a ring. Color never decorates a button.

    R01 R10 R19 R21 R32

  4. 04

    The dot builds everything

    Mascot, loaders, dividers, glyphs and texture. The only gradient is quantized: one solid color per dot or stripe.

    R10 R11 R12 R20 R29

  5. 05

    The character lives in a frame

    The dot T-rex stays inside a screen: the chat card, the runner, a scene. Around it, smooth type and hairlines.

    R04 R11 R13 R14

  6. 06

    Big, tight, calm type

    Geist display at -0.045em and line-height 1. Mono uppercase labels are the system's voice. Korean gets air.

    R01 R04 R07 R15 R34

  7. 07

    Emphasis in two tones

    The claim in ink, the continuation in gray, at one size. No colored or gradient words in headlines.

    R01 R05 R09 R23 R32

  8. 08

    Structure from hairlines

    1px rings and a visible grid of dots. Two shadow levels, float and pop, only for what leaves the page; shadows never separate sections.

    R03 R06 R07 R09 R28

  9. 09

    Controls on a rhythm

    36, 44 and 52 px heights, one radius family, 16px input text and a 2px focus ring at 2px.

    R01 R02 R03 R09 R10

  10. 10

    Motion with intent

    UI moves in 120 to 320 ms; characters spring. Only transform and opacity. Nothing loops in the reading zone.

    R01 R04 R15 R18 R35

  11. 11

    120 fps is a design rule

    No video, blur or WebGL. Scenes pause offscreen; will-change only while something moves.

    R05 R09 R11 R25 R26

  12. 12

    Show the agent at work

    Request, steps that turn from present to past with a check, the answer, a mono receipt. Decisions as button pairs.

    R05 R25 R30 R31 R32

  13. 13

    Autoplay with manners

    Every loop has a visible pause, stops on hover, focus and offscreen, and shows its final frame under reduced motion.

    R15 R17 R26 R35

  14. 14

    Honest forms, plain words

    Visible labels, required consent, a honeypot, errors that keep the input, and one true trust line.

    R03 R23 R31 R33 R34

  15. 15

    One character, many states

    The T-rex changes only its 3x3 eye cell and props. A state machine with time limits drives it.

02 Color

Paper and ink. Five signals, one at a time.

Twelve neutrals from one cool hue carry the page. The brand stops are flat fills with a deep variant for text and a tint for fills. Ratios below are computed live from the shipped tokens.

Neutral scale

OKLCH hue 170, chroma at most 0.0065. Ratio against --n-1 paper.

Signals

Solid for dots and fills behind ink. Deep for text (at least 4.8:1 on paper, white and its tint). Tint and soft for fills.

The signature: five flat stripes, mint to indigo. Use it sparingly: the logo lockup, the runner's top edge, a success state.

Semantic roles

Components use roles, never raw steps. [data-signal] rescopes --accent for a section.

Contrast

Every text and UI pair the system defines. Text needs 4.5:1, large text and boundaries 3:1. Also checked in CI-able form: bun src/client/styleguide/contrast-report.ts.

04 Space

A 4px base. Generous between, tight within.

Fourteen steps for components, plus two fluid tokens for page rhythm: --space-section (80 to 144) and --space-stack (32 to 56).

05 Shape

Six radii and a pill. Hairlines, never heavy borders.

Controls are pills, echoing the dot. Containers step up with their size. Lines are 1px insets so they never move layout.

Lines

Decorative hairlines may be faint. Anything that identifies a control meets 3:1.

--line hairline, cards

--line-strong buttons, kbd

--line-ui inputs, 3:1

--focus 2px at 2px

08 Motion

Short and precise. Springy where a character moves.

Five durations, four curves and three springs written as linear(). Only transform and opacity animate. Under reduced motion every duration becomes zero; demos started with Play still run.

Durations

On --ease-out.

Curves and springs

Plot over time, then the same curve moving a dot.

Stagger and assembly

--stagger 50ms between items; the T-rex assembles tail to head on --dur-character.

Animate

transform, opacity. Visibility flips at the end of a fade.

Never animate

width, height, top, left, margins, grid tracks, filters, shadows, clip-path, backdrop-filter.

Reduced motion

Durations to 0, no parallax, assembly, marquee or autoplay. Final states show; Play still works.

Budget

About 10 running animations outside the active scene. will-change only while moving.

09 The dot

One dot, from texture to character.

The TUI draws the T-rex in braille dots; the web draws the same grid as round dots, never square pixels. Texture stays sparse and faint.

States in the eye cell

Only the 3x3 eye cell (outlined) and the mouth change. Each state is shown with its head enlarged above the full specimen. Body dots step through the five stops by column, tail to head.

Dot glyphs

Icons on a 5x5 grid of the same dot. Names render as text beside them; no third-party logos.

10 Dinosaur themes

Four dinosaurs, one grid and one set of rules.

A theme is a species (ADR 0100): the T-rex (Signal, the default), the pterodactyl (Dusk), the brachiosaurus (Fern) and the triceratops (Canyon). Each brings its own 30x18 bitmap, five body stops and an accent family. Status colors never change: running stays amber, done mint, failed coral, waiting indigo.

What a theme changes

<html data-dino> swaps the mascot (every mounted one, the runner, the head marks) and points --accent, --accent-deep, --accent-tint, --accent-soft and --selection at the species' family. Text, surfaces, [data-signal] chapters, --success and --danger stay put. The choice is kept in localStorage as dn-dino.
